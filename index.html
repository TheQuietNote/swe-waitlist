What Is SWE Studio

Not a marketplace.
A creative network
with a pipeline.

SWE Studio is the creative services arm of SunriseWithEnoh. We exist to solve two problems at once — helping clients find reliable, high-quality creative talent, and helping African creatives build sustainable careers in the literary industry.

Start a Project
Curated, not open
Creatives apply to join. They're assessed on both current skills and potential. Not everyone gets in — and that's the point.
We manage the process
From matching to delivery, we oversee quality at every stage. Clients don't deal with multiple freelancers — they deal with us.
Emerging creatives aren't turned away
If you're talented but early in your career, we don't reject you. We give you a structured path to get there — mock projects, mentorship, portfolio building.
Built specifically for literary work
Our network understands the industry — the craft, the context, the standards that publishing actually requires.
The Pipeline Model

We don't just find talent.
We create it.

This is what makes SWE Studio different. A new creative joins, develops through structured learning, progresses through quality levels, and eventually works on major client projects. The pipeline is always growing — and so is the talent inside it.
